Multiway rules of thumb

Your fair share shrinks
With 5 opponents you only "deserve" 1/6 of the pot. One pair, even JJ, is often behind the field once the money goes in.
Passive players' big bets are real
A full-pot lead from a Station or Nit is mostly two pair, sets or big draws. Fold one-pair hands more than feels right.
Count who's behind you
Players still to act can raise, so calling needs more than the pot odds say.
Bet big when you're best
Live players don't fold. When you're ahead on a wet board, charge every draw; with a medium hand, check.
