    Basic strategy

    Pre-flop · bet 4×
    Any pair 3s and up · any ace · any suited king, K5 offsuit and up · Q6 suited and up, Q8 offsuit and up · J8 suited and up, JT offsuit. Never bet 3×. Otherwise check.
    Flop · bet 2×
    Two pair or better · any pair using your own card, except pocket 2s · four to a flush with a 10 or better of that suit in your hand. Otherwise check.
    River · bet 1× or fold
    Bet any made hand that uses your cards, or when fewer than 21 cards beat you as one of the dealer's. Otherwise fold.
